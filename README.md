### PCB design and hardware development

This account maintains open resources from **Circuit Board Design**, the PCB design division of **Electuni LLC** (a US company founded in 2016).

- **[circuit-board-design.com](https://www.circuit-board-design.com)**: schematic capture, multilayer PCB layout, DFM/DFT review and pre-fab design review in Altium Designer, KiCad and Cadence Allegro
- **[electuni.com](https://www.electuni.com)**: full-stack hardware development, from first prototype through EVT/DVT/PVT to manufacturing support
